Storevane

Operations agent for store chains

Find what is going wrong in your stores before the day is over

Storevane reads your sales, stock and complaints, finds the problems that cost the most money, and drafts the fix for each one. Nothing is sent until a person approves it.

First time here, or judging? Press “Try it with demo data”. It loads made-up data for three UAE stores and runs the agent. Then open “Approve and send” to see the human approval step.

Use my own data

1. Your data

Paste from Excel or Google Sheets, or upload a CSV. The first line must be the column names. Nothing is stored. Your data stays on this page.

Type any 3-letter code. INR groups digits the Indian way (1,09,300).
date, store, sales, target, footfall, transactions, staff_scheduled, staff_present
store, sku, product, on_hand, avg_daily_sales, lead_days, unit_price, supplier, supplier_email (optional)
store, date, issue, hours_open. Do not include customer names or phone numbers.
Or load from a Google Sheet link

In Google Sheets choose File, Share, Publish to web, pick the tab and CSV, and paste the link. Anyone who has a published link can read it, so do not include customer names or phone numbers. An ordinary sheet link works only if sharing is set to “anyone with the link can view”.
